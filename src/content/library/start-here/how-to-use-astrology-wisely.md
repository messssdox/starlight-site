## What these traditions are good for

At their best, astrology and its cousins give you a language for talking about yourself and the people you love. A reading can put words to something you have always felt, help you see a difficult relationship from the other person's side, or simply give you a moment to think about where you are heading. Many people find that valuable, and that is a perfectly good reason to enjoy them.

## What they are not

Scientific studies have not found that the position of the planets at birth predicts personality or events. Starlight is honest about that. Treat readings as reflection and storytelling, not as facts or forecasts, and never use them to make medical, legal or financial decisions.

## A few gentle rules

- **Take what fits, leave what does not.** If a description does not ring true, trust yourself over the reading.
- **No sign is good or bad.** Every sign, animal and number has gifts and growing edges.
- **A clash is not a curse.** Challenging patterns in a chart or on the wheel point to growth, and many happy couples have them.
- **You are more than a birthday.** Upbringing, choices, culture and luck shape you far more than any chart.
- **Be kind with it.** Never use a sign to judge or exclude someone.
