## The four kinds of match

- **Friend group (same colour on the wheel):** natural understanding and shared values. One of the best matches.
- **Secret friend (matching jewel on the inner ring):** a quiet, loyal bond that often surprises people. Also one of the best.
- **Clash (directly opposite):** opposite energies. The most challenging, but full of growth.
- **Everyone else:** a neutral match that depends on the people and on the rest of the reading.

The full explanation is in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Same animal

Two people of the same animal understand each other instantly, sometimes too well: they share the same strengths and the same blind spots. Many astrologers see it as a good match that needs a little outside perspective.

## Elements add another layer

The year elements matter too. A Wood person and a Fire person sit on the feeding cycle (Wood feeds Fire), which is supportive; a Water person and a Fire person sit on the controlling cycle, which can mean friction. See [The five elements (Wu Xing)](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing).
