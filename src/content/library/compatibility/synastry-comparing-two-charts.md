## What synastry is

In synastry, an astrologer places one person's planets around the other person's chart and looks at the [aspects](/library/houses-aspects/the-major-aspects) between them. Each connection describes part of the relationship: attraction, friendship, friction, growth.

## Where to start

- **Sun and Moon:** one person's Sun in harmony with the other's Moon is a classic sign of feeling understood.
- **Venus and Mars:** contacts between these show attraction and chemistry.
- **Mercury:** shows how easily you talk and understand each other.
- **Saturn:** contacts with Saturn can add commitment and staying power, or feel heavy.
- **Elements:** sharing elements (or complementary ones, like Fire with Air) helps things flow. See [Fire, Earth, Air and Water](/library/elements/fire-earth-air-and-water).

## Without birth times

Even without birth times, you can compare Sun, Venus, Mars and usually Moon signs, which already says a lot. With birth times, the houses and Rising signs add depth.

## Composite charts

Some astrologers also make a composite chart, a single chart for the relationship itself, built from the midpoints between the two people's planets. It describes the relationship as its own living thing.
