## The three number families

| Family | Numbers | What they share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent minds | 1, 5, 7 | need for freedom, thinking for themselves |
| Steady builders | 2, 4, 8 (and 11, 22) | love of security, structure and commitment |
| Creative hearts | 3, 6, 9 (and 33) | warmth, expression and care for others |

Two people from the same family usually "get" each other quickly, because they want similar things from life.

## Across families

Matches between families are not bad: they are complementary. A 1 (independent) with a 6 (caring) can make a wonderful team if the 1 values the 6's warmth and the 6 gives the 1 room to lead. The key is to understand that you are motivated by different things.

## Same number

Two people with the same Life Path understand each other deeply but can share the same blind spots. Two 8s may both want to be in charge; two 2s may both wait for the other to decide.

## Beyond the Life Path

For a fuller picture, numerologists also compare Soul Urge numbers (what each person longs for) and Expression numbers (how each person goes about things). A couple with different Life Paths but matching Soul Urges often feel a deep, unspoken bond.
