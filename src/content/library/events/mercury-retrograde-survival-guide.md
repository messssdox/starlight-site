## What it is

Three or four times a year, for about three weeks, Mercury appears to move backwards through the zodiac. It is an optical illusion caused by Earth and Mercury moving at different speeds. The full explanation is in [Retrograde planets](/library/moon-sky/retrograde-planets).

## Why astrologers pay attention

Mercury rules communication, travel, contracts and technology, so astrologers traditionally see its retrograde as a time when those areas need extra care: messages go astray, plans change, gadgets misbehave. There is no scientific evidence for this, but many people find it a useful reminder to slow down.

## The "re" rule

Mercury retrograde is a good time for anything starting with "re":

- **Review** plans and documents before you sign them.
- **Revise** that half-finished project.
- **Reconnect** with old friends; people from the past often get in touch.
- **Rest** and reflect before the next push forward.
- **Back up** your files, and leave a little extra time for journeys.

## The shadow periods

Astrologers also talk about the "shadow" for a couple of weeks either side of a retrograde, when Mercury is passing over the same stretch of sky. Things set in motion in the shadow may come up again during the retrograde.

## Keep it in proportion

Life does not stop for three weeks. Babies are born, weddings happen and businesses launch during every Mercury retrograde, and many go wonderfully. If something goes wrong, it is probably just life.
