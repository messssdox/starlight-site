## The story of the Dog

The Dog was a strong swimmer, but it loved the water so much that it stopped to play and bathe in the river during the Great Race. It came eleventh, happy and clean.

## Personality

Dogs are loyal, sincere and deeply fair. They have a strong sense of right and wrong and will defend friends and causes with everything they have. They are good listeners and trustworthy keepers of secrets.

## In love

The Dog is sincere and devoted in love, giving their whole heart to the right person. They need honesty and reassurance, and in return they offer a love that lasts.

## At work

Dogs are drawn to work with meaning: law, teaching, healthcare, social work and community service. They are happiest when they know their work helps someone.

## Something to watch

The Dog can be anxious and quick to expect the worst. Learning to rest and trust that things may turn out well is the Dog's great gift to itself.

## Friends, secret friend and clash

- **Friend group:** the Dog shares its sunflower yellow colour on the wheel with the Tiger and the Horse. Together they are the Protectors, and they understand each other easily.
- **Secret friend:** the Rabbit. This quiet, loyal bond is shown by a matching jewel on the inner ring.
- **Clash:** the Dragon, directly opposite on the wheel. The two pull in opposite directions, which can mean friction, or real growth if both are willing to learn.

You can read more in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Element and season

The Dog's own fixed element is **Earth**, and it is a **Yang** sign. It belongs to autumn on the wheel. Every Dog year also carries a year element, which changes in a ten-year cycle, so a Fire Dog and a Water Dog share the same heart but show it differently. See [The five elements](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing) for how the elements work.

## Lucky things

Traditionally, the Dog's lucky numbers are 3, 4 and 9, its lucky colours are red, green and purple, and its lucky flowers are rose and cymbidium orchid.
