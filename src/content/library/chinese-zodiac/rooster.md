## The story of the Rooster

The Rooster found the raft that carried it, the Goat and the Monkey across the river in the Great Race, and came tenth. Being the one who spots the solution is very Rooster.

## Personality

Roosters are observant, organised and confident. They notice every detail, speak their mind and hold themselves to high standards. They are hard-working, punctual and often surprisingly brave.

## In love

The Rooster is faithful and protective in love, showing care through dependability and practical help. They appreciate a partner who is honest with them and does not take offence at their frankness.

## At work

Roosters excel where detail matters: accounting, medicine, journalism, planning, the military and any role that needs precision.

## Something to watch

The Rooster can be blunt or critical, both of others and of itself. Softening the delivery, while keeping the honesty, wins the Rooster many more friends.

## Friends, secret friend and clash

- **Friend group:** the Rooster shares its rose pink colour on the wheel with the Ox and the Snake. Together they are the Builders, and they understand each other easily.
- **Secret friend:** the Dragon. This quiet, loyal bond is shown by a matching jewel on the inner ring.
- **Clash:** the Rabbit, directly opposite on the wheel. The two pull in opposite directions, which can mean friction, or real growth if both are willing to learn.

You can read more in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Element and season

The Rooster's own fixed element is **Metal**, and it is a **Yin** sign. It belongs to autumn on the wheel. Every Rooster year also carries a year element, which changes in a ten-year cycle, so a Fire Rooster and a Water Rooster share the same heart but show it differently. See [The five elements](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing) for how the elements work.

## Lucky things

Traditionally, the Rooster's lucky numbers are 5, 7 and 8, its lucky colours are gold, brown and yellow, and its lucky flowers are gladiolus and cockscomb.
