## The story of the Tiger

The Tiger came third in the Great Race, fighting its way across the strong currents of the river by sheer power and determination. It arrived soaked and exhausted, but proud, which is exactly how Tigers like to win.

## Personality

Tigers are bold, energetic and full of conviction. They stand up for the underdog, chase big dreams and are rarely boring company. People are drawn to their confidence, and they make natural leaders and campaigners.

## In love

In love, the Tiger is passionate, generous and wholehearted. They fall quickly and love deeply, but they need room to breathe. The best partner for a Tiger is one who admires their spirit without trying to tame it.

## At work

Tigers thrive in leadership, adventure, activism, sport, entrepreneurship and anywhere that needs someone brave enough to go first. Routine work tends to make them restless.

## Something to watch

The Tiger can act on impulse and struggles to take advice. Pausing before leaping, and finishing what they start, turns their courage into lasting success.

## Friends, secret friend and clash

- **Friend group:** the Tiger shares its sunflower yellow colour on the wheel with the Horse and the Dog. Together they are the Protectors, and they understand each other easily.
- **Secret friend:** the Pig. This quiet, loyal bond is shown by a matching jewel on the inner ring.
- **Clash:** the Monkey, directly opposite on the wheel. The two pull in opposite directions, which can mean friction, or real growth if both are willing to learn.

You can read more in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Element and season

The Tiger's own fixed element is **Wood**, and it is a **Yang** sign. It belongs to spring on the wheel. Every Tiger year also carries a year element, which changes in a ten-year cycle, so a Fire Tiger and a Water Tiger share the same heart but show it differently. See [The five elements](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing) for how the elements work.

## Lucky things

Traditionally, the Tiger's lucky numbers are 1, 3 and 4, its lucky colours are blue, grey and orange, and its lucky flowers are cineraria.
