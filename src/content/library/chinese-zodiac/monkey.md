## The story of the Monkey

In the Great Race the Monkey teamed up with the Goat and the Rooster, using its quick hands to steer their raft across the river. It came ninth, having enjoyed every minute.

## Personality

Monkeys are bright, funny and endlessly inventive. They love learning, games and anything with a puzzle at its heart. Their quick humour makes them excellent company, and they rarely meet a problem they cannot think their way around.

## In love

In love, the Monkey is playful and lively. They need a partner who keeps up with their mind and laughs at their jokes, and they show love by making life an adventure.

## At work

Monkeys are brilliant in science, technology, engineering, trading, entertainment and any role built around solving problems.

## Something to watch

The Monkey can get bored easily or rely on cleverness instead of patience. Sticking with things after the novelty wears off lets their talent go much further.

## Friends, secret friend and clash

- **Friend group:** the Monkey shares its sea green colour on the wheel with the Rat and the Dragon. Together they are the Thinkers, and they understand each other easily.
- **Secret friend:** the Snake. This quiet, loyal bond is shown by a matching jewel on the inner ring.
- **Clash:** the Tiger, directly opposite on the wheel. The two pull in opposite directions, which can mean friction, or real growth if both are willing to learn.

You can read more in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Element and season

The Monkey's own fixed element is **Metal**, and it is a **Yang** sign. It belongs to autumn on the wheel. Every Monkey year also carries a year element, which changes in a ten-year cycle, so a Fire Monkey and a Water Monkey share the same heart but show it differently. See [The five elements](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing) for how the elements work.

## Lucky things

Traditionally, the Monkey's lucky numbers are 4 and 9, its lucky colours are white, blue and gold, and its lucky flowers are chrysanthemum and crape myrtle.
