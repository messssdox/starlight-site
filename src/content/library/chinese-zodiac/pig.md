## The story of the Pig

The Pig came last in the Great Race because it stopped for a feast and a nap on the way. It arrived very contented, and the Jade Emperor still gave it a place on the wheel.

## Personality

Pigs are warm, generous and good-humoured. They enjoy life's pleasures, from good food to good company, and love sharing them. They are honest and trusting, and they see the best in people.

## In love

The Pig is affectionate and easy-going in love, creating a comfortable, loving home. They are loyal partners who forgive easily and love to spoil the people they care about.

## At work

Pigs thrive in hospitality, food, the arts, charity work and anything that helps people. Their kindness and patience make them valued colleagues.

## Something to watch

The Pig can be too trusting, or indulge a little too much. Saying no now and then, and protecting its own generosity, keeps the Pig happy.

## Friends, secret friend and clash

- **Friend group:** the Pig shares its slate blue colour on the wheel with the Rabbit and the Goat. Together they are the Diplomats, and they understand each other easily.
- **Secret friend:** the Tiger. This quiet, loyal bond is shown by a matching jewel on the inner ring.
- **Clash:** the Snake, directly opposite on the wheel. The two pull in opposite directions, which can mean friction, or real growth if both are willing to learn.

You can read more in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Element and season

The Pig's own fixed element is **Water**, and it is a **Yin** sign. It belongs to winter on the wheel. Every Pig year also carries a year element, which changes in a ten-year cycle, so a Fire Pig and a Water Pig share the same heart but show it differently. See [The five elements](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing) for how the elements work.

## Lucky things

Traditionally, the Pig's lucky numbers are 2, 5 and 8, its lucky colours are yellow, grey, brown and gold, and its lucky flowers are hydrangea and daisy.

## The Pig or the Boar?

In many English-language books this sign is called the Boar, and in Japan the twelfth animal is the wild boar. The Chinese character can mean either. On Starlight we use Pig, but the personality is the same whichever name you prefer.
