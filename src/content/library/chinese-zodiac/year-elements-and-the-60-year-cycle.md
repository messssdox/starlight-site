## Two elements for every animal

Each animal has its own fixed element: the Rat is always Water at heart, the Horse always Fire. On top of that, every year has a **year element** that changes in a regular pattern. Your year element colours how your animal's character comes out.

## Finding the year element from the last digit

The year element follows the last digit of the zodiac year:

| Last digit | Element |
| --- | --- |
| 0 or 1 | Metal |
| 2 or 3 | Water |
| 4 or 5 | Wood |
| 6 or 7 | Fire |
| 8 or 9 | Earth |

So 1990 is a Metal Horse, 2002 a Water Horse and 2026 a Fire Horse. Remember to use the zodiac year: someone born in January before Lunar New Year belongs to the year before.

## How the element changes your animal

- **Wood** adds warmth, generosity and growth. Wood animals are idealistic and kind.
- **Fire** adds passion, drive and charisma. Fire animals are bold and expressive.
- **Earth** adds steadiness and common sense. Earth animals are practical and dependable.
- **Metal** adds determination and principle. Metal animals are focused and strong-willed.
- **Water** adds intuition and flexibility. Water animals are thoughtful and persuasive.

## The great cycle of sixty

Twelve animals and five elements (each used for two years in a row, once yang and once yin) line up again only every sixty years. That is why a Fire Horse year like 2026 comes round only once in a lifetime for most people: the last was 1966 and the next will be 2086. Reaching your sixtieth birthday is traditionally celebrated as completing a full cycle.
