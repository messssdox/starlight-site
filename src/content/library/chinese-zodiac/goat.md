## The story of the Goat

The Goat crossed the river in the Great Race together with the Monkey and the Rooster, who worked as a team to find a raft and clear a path through the weeds. The Goat came eighth, a reminder that it does its best work with friends around it.

## Personality

Goats are gentle, imaginative and deeply kind. They have a strong sense of beauty and often a gift for music, art or making a home feel lovely. They care about how others feel and are happiest when everyone is getting along.

## In love

The Goat is romantic and sensitive in love. They thrive with a protective, encouraging partner who appreciates their tenderness and gives them a sense of security.

## At work

Goats are gifted in art, music, design, gardening, writing and caring professions. They do best in a supportive team rather than a cut-throat competition.

## Something to watch

The Goat can worry, doubt itself and lean on others for reassurance. Trusting its own talent, which is considerable, is the Goat's lesson.

## Friends, secret friend and clash

- **Friend group:** the Goat shares its slate blue colour on the wheel with the Rabbit and the Pig. Together they are the Diplomats, and they understand each other easily.
- **Secret friend:** the Horse. This quiet, loyal bond is shown by a matching jewel on the inner ring.
- **Clash:** the Ox, directly opposite on the wheel. The two pull in opposite directions, which can mean friction, or real growth if both are willing to learn.

You can read more in [Friend groups, secret friends and clashes](/library/chinese-zodiac/friend-groups-secret-friends-and-clashes).

## Element and season

The Goat's own fixed element is **Earth**, and it is a **Yin** sign. It belongs to summer on the wheel. Every Goat year also carries a year element, which changes in a ten-year cycle, so a Fire Goat and a Water Goat share the same heart but show it differently. See [The five elements](/library/elements/the-five-elements-wu-xing) for how the elements work.

## Lucky things

Traditionally, the Goat's lucky numbers are 2 and 7, its lucky colours are brown, red and purple, and its lucky flowers are carnation and primrose.
