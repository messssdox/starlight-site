## What Ben Ming Nian means

Ben Ming Nian (本命年) means "origin year": the year of the animal you were born under. You meet it at ages 12, 24, 36, 48, 60 and so on. 2026 is a Horse year, so it is Ben Ming Nian for everyone born in a Horse year.

## Why it is seen as a tricky year

Traditionally your own animal's year is thought to offend Tai Sui, the god of the year, which can bring upheaval. In practice, people describe it as a year when life asks you to grow up a little: changes at work or home, decisions you have been putting off, a sense of being tested.

## Red for luck

The best-known custom is to wear something red throughout the year, especially red underwear, socks or a red string bracelet, ideally given to you by someone who loves you. Red is the colour of protection and good fortune in Chinese culture.

## A gentle way to approach it

- Move carefully with very big, risky decisions.
- Look after your health and rest well.
- Use the year to finish old business and set solid foundations.
- Remember that many people have wonderful Ben Ming Nian years. It is a year of turning points, and turning points can be good.
