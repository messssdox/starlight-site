## Your chart is fixed, the sky keeps moving

Your birth chart is a snapshot. But the planets carry on moving, and as they pass over important points in your chart, astrologers say they "trigger" them. These moving planets are called transits.

## Fast and slow transits

- **The Moon** changes sign every couple of days and colours your mood for a day or two.
- **The Sun, Mercury, Venus and Mars** bring themes that last days or weeks, such as the Sun returning to its birth place on your birthday.
- **Jupiter and Saturn** bring chapters of a year or two, like the famous Saturn return around age 29.
- **Uranus, Neptune and Pluto** bring the great, slow changes of a lifetime.

## Reading a transit

Use the same recipe as for a birth chart: the transiting planet (what), the aspect it makes (how) and the house or planet it touches (where). For example, Jupiter crossing your tenth house can mean a year of opportunity in your career.
