## The heart of 2

Two is the number of partnership. Twos sense what others feel, bring people together and make wonderful friends, partners and mediators. They notice details others miss and work best as part of a pair or a team.

## In love

In love, Twos are tender, romantic and devoted. Harmony at home matters enormously to them.

## At work

Twos excel in counselling, diplomacy, teaching, music and any role that needs tact and teamwork.

## Something to watch

Twos can put everyone else first and lose sight of their own needs. Their lesson is to value themselves as much as they value harmony.

## 2 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of patience and partnership.

## Who 2 gets on with

Two feels most at home with 4 and 8, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
