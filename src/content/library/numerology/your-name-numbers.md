## Which name to use

Use your full name as it was first registered at birth, including any middle names. Numerologists see it as your original blueprint. If you have changed your name, try both: your current name often describes how you show up in the world today.

## Expression (or Destiny): every letter

Add up every letter in your name and reduce. Your Expression number describes your natural talents and the way you tend to go about things.

**Example, Grace Ellen Hart:** G7 R9 A1 C3 E5 + E5 L3 L3 E5 N5 + H8 A1 R9 T2 = 66, then 6 + 6 = 12, then 1 + 2 = **3**. Grace's Expression is 3, the Creator.

## Soul Urge (or Heart's Desire): the vowels

Add up only the vowels (A, E, I, O, U). This number describes what you want most deep down, the thing that makes you feel fulfilled.

**Grace Ellen Hart:** A1 E5 E5 E5 A1 = 17, then 1 + 7 = **8**. Deep down, Grace wants achievement and security.

## Personality: the consonants

Add up only the consonants. This is the side of you that other people meet first, like the cover of a book.

**Grace Ellen Hart:** G7 R9 C3 L3 L3 N5 H8 R9 T2 = 49, then 4 + 9 = 13, then 1 + 3 = **4**. People first see Grace as steady and dependable.

## What about Y?

Some numerologists count Y as a vowel when it sounds like one, as in "Lynn". Starlight keeps it simple and always treats Y as a consonant, so the result is the same every time.

## Reading them together

When your three name numbers and your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number) agree, you probably feel very much yourself. When they differ, as Grace's 3, 8 and 4 do, it simply means you have several sides, and life is about letting each one have its say.
