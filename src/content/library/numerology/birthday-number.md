## What it is

Your Birthday number is one of the five core numbers in numerology, alongside your Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge and Personality. It is the simplest of them all: it comes only from the day of the month you were born.

Where your Life Path describes the whole road you walk, your Birthday number describes **one particular gift** you bring to it. Many numerologists call it a talent you can lean on when the road gets hard.

## How to work it out

Take the day you were born and reduce it to a single digit. Days 11 and 22 are master numbers, so they are kept as they are.

**Examples:**

- Born on the 7th: your Birthday number is **7**.
- Born on the 14th: 1 + 4 = **5**.
- Born on the 29th: 2 + 9 = 11, a master number, so it stays **11**.
- Born on the 22nd: a master number, so it stays **22**.

## Why the day matters

Your birth year is shared with everyone your age and your month with a twelfth of the world, but the day is the most personal part of your birth date. Numerology gives it a number of its own for that reason.

Some numerologists also look at the day before it is reduced. Someone born on the 23rd has the 5 gift (2 + 3) with a flavour of 2 and 3 in it, which adds sensitivity and creativity to the freedom of the 5.

## Reading your Birthday number

The meaning of each number is the same one you meet everywhere in numerology: a 1 brings independence, a 6 brings care, a 9 brings compassion, and so on. See the numbers 1 to 9 in the Library for each one. Read your Birthday number as the gift version of that number: a strength that comes easily to you and that you can offer others.
