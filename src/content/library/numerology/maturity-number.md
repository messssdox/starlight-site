## What it is

Your Maturity number (sometimes called the Realisation number) describes the person you are growing into, and the goal your life quietly builds towards. Numerologists say you start to feel it in your thirties, and that it shapes the second half of life more and more.

It joins two other numbers: your **Life Path**, the road you walk, and your **Expression**, the talents you bring to it. Put them together and you get where the road and the talents lead.

## How to work it out

Add your Life Path and your Expression numbers, then reduce the total. Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are kept.

**Example:** a Life Path 9 and an Expression 3. 9 + 3 = 12, then 1 + 2 = **3**. This person grows into a 3, the Creator: their later years are likely to bring more creativity, self-expression and joy.

You need your full birth name for this number, because the Expression comes from your name.

## Reading your Maturity number

Use the meaning of the number itself (see the numbers 1 to 9 in the Library) and read it as **where you are heading**:

- If your Maturity number matches your Life Path or Expression, that energy grows stronger as you get older.
- If it is different, it adds something new: a side of you that may feel unfamiliar when you are young and natural later on.

Many people find their Maturity number makes most sense looking back, so it is worth revisiting every few years.
