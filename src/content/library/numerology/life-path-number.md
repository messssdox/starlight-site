## How to work it out

Reduce the day, the month and the year of your birth separately, then add the three results and reduce again. Here is an example for 14 July 1990:

- Day 14: 1 + 4 = **5**
- Month 7: already one digit, so **7**
- Year 1990: 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19, then 1 + 9 = 10, then 1 + 0 = **1**
- Add them: 5 + 7 + 1 = 13, then 1 + 3 = **4**

So someone born on 14 July 1990 has Life Path 4.

If you reach 11, 22 or 33 at any stage, keep it: these are [master numbers](/library/numerology/master-numbers). For example, 1 January 1980 gives 1 + 1 + 9 = **11**.

## What it means

Your Life Path is like the road you are walking. It describes the qualities that come naturally, the kind of work and relationships that feel right, and the lessons that keep turning up until you learn them.

## Life Path and other people

Numerologists group the numbers into three families that tend to understand each other: **1, 5 and 7** (independent minds), **2, 4 and 8** (steady builders) and **3, 6 and 9** (creative hearts). See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
