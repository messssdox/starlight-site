## The heart of 6

Six is the number of love and home. Sixes look after people and create beauty and comfort wherever they are. They have a strong sense of duty and fairness.

## In love

In love, Sixes are devoted, romantic and family-minded, often the heart of their household.

## At work

Sixes flourish in healthcare, teaching, design, hospitality and community work.

## Something to watch

Sixes can take on too much or try to fix everyone. Their lesson is to care for themselves as generously as for others.

## 6 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of home, love and responsibility.

## Who 6 gets on with

Six feels most at home with 3 and 9, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).

## The master number 33

Thirty-three reduces to 6 and is its heightened form. If your number is 33, read this article and [Master numbers](/library/numerology/master-numbers).
