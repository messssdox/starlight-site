## How to work it out

Add your birth day, your birth month and the current year, reducing each one, then reduce the total. Master numbers are not kept here.

**Example:** a birthday of 14 July, in 2026.

- Day 14: 1 + 4 = 5
- Month 7: 7
- Year 2026: 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, then 1 + 0 = 1
- 5 + 7 + 1 = 13, then 1 + 3 = **4**

So for this person 2026 is a Personal Year 4. Most numerologists count the year from 1 January; some count it from your birthday. Starlight uses 1 January.

## The nine-year cycle

1. **Beginnings.** Plant seeds: start projects, make plans, be brave.
2. **Patience.** Things grow quietly. Focus on partnerships, cooperation and rest.
3. **Expression.** A sociable, creative year. Share your ideas and enjoy yourself.
4. **Foundations.** Hard work, organisation and building something solid.
5. **Change.** Freedom, travel, surprises and a welcome shake-up.
6. **Home and heart.** Family, love, responsibility and caring for others.
7. **Reflection.** A quieter year for study, rest, faith and finding answers within.
8. **Harvest.** Achievement, recognition, money matters and stepping into your power.
9. **Completion.** Finish, forgive and clear space for the new cycle ahead.

## The Universal Year

The calendar year has a number for everyone, too. 2026 adds up to 1 (2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, then 1), so 2026 is a Universal Year 1: a fresh start for the world as a whole.
