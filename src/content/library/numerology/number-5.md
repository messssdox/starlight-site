## The heart of 5

Five is the number of freedom. Fives love variety, travel, people and new ideas, and they learn by experience. They are quick, clever and great fun.

## In love

In love, Fives are exciting and passionate, but need a partner who gives them room to roam.

## At work

Fives do well in travel, sales, media, journalism and any job with change and movement.

## Something to watch

Fives can be restless and impulsive. Their lesson is to find freedom that still has roots.

## 5 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of change and freedom.

## Who 5 gets on with

Five feels most at home with 1 and 7, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
