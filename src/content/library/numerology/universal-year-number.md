## What it is

Every calendar year has a number of its own. Numerologists read it like the weather of the year: a mood that everyone lives in together. Your **Personal Year** is how that weather feels for you, and it is the more important of the two for your own reading.

## How to work it out

Add the digits of the year and reduce to a single digit. Master numbers are not kept here.

**Examples:**

- 2026: 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, then 1 + 0 = **1**.
- 2027: 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, then 1 + 1 = **2**.
- 2028: 2 + 0 + 2 + 8 = 12, then 1 + 2 = **3**.

## The years ahead

The Universal Year follows the same nine-year cycle as your Personal Year:

1. **Beginnings:** new ideas and fresh starts for the world.
2. **Cooperation:** partnerships, patience and diplomacy.
3. **Expression:** creativity, communication and celebration.
4. **Building:** hard work, structure and steady progress.
5. **Change:** movement, freedom and surprises.
6. **Care:** home, community and responsibility.
7. **Reflection:** study, questioning and looking within.
8. **Power:** money, ambition and results.
9. **Completion:** endings, clearing out and making room.

2026 is a Universal Year 1, so the world is starting a new nine-year cycle. Your own Personal Year tells you how that fresh start lands for you.
