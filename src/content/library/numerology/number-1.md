## The heart of 1

One is the number of beginnings. Ones are natural leaders who trust their own ideas and like to go first. They are courageous, determined and full of initiative, often happiest when they are their own boss.

## In love

In love, Ones are loyal and protective, but they need a partner who respects their independence and does not try to steer.

## At work

Ones shine in leadership, entrepreneurship, invention and anything they can pioneer.

## Something to watch

Ones can become stubborn or lonely if they insist on doing everything alone. Their lesson is to lead with confidence while letting others in.

## 1 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of beginnings.

## Who 1 gets on with

One feels most at home with 5 and 7, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
