## What they are

If your Life Path is the road you walk, your Challenge Numbers are the hills on it. They point to the lessons you are here to work through, and the strengths you build by working through them. A challenge is not a weakness or a punishment: it is where your biggest growth tends to happen.

You have four, one for each stage of life:

- **First Challenge:** early life.
- **Second Challenge:** the middle years.
- **Third Challenge:** your main challenge, with you all through life.
- **Fourth Challenge:** later life.

## How to work them out

Reduce your birth day, month and year to single digits. Master numbers are reduced here too. Then take the **gap** between pairs of them, always subtracting the smaller from the larger:

- **First:** month and day.
- **Second:** day and year.
- **Third:** the First and Second Challenges.
- **Fourth:** month and year.

**Example, 14 March 1990:** day 1 + 4 = 5, month 3, year 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19, then 1 + 9 = 10, then 1. First 5 − 3 = **2**, Second 5 − 1 = **4**, Third 4 − 2 = **2**, Fourth 3 − 1 = **2**.

Because you always subtract, a challenge can only be 0 to 8. There is no 9 challenge.

## What each one means

- **0, The Choice:** no single lesson is set, so you choose which to work on.
- **1, Standing Tall:** trusting yourself and saying what you need.
- **2, Sensitivity:** not taking things too personally.
- **3, Self-Expression:** sharing real feelings instead of hiding them.
- **4, Discipline:** steady habits, with room to enjoy the journey.
- **5, Freedom:** handling change without running from commitment.
- **6, Responsibility:** caring without controlling.
- **7, Faith:** trusting other people and life itself.
- **8, Money and Power:** a balanced relationship with success and control.

When the same number appears more than once, as the 2 does in the example, that lesson is especially strong for you.
