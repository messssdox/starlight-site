## The heart of 7

Seven is the number of inner wisdom. Sevens ask deep questions and need quiet time to find answers. They are thoughtful, analytical and often drawn to science, philosophy or spirituality.

## In love

In love, Sevens are loyal and deep, but need a partner who respects their need for solitude.

## At work

Sevens excel in research, science, writing, analysis, and spiritual or healing work.

## Something to watch

Sevens can withdraw or become distant. Their lesson is to trust their intuition and share what they discover.

## 7 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of reflection and study.

## Who 7 gets on with

Seven feels most at home with 1 and 5, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
