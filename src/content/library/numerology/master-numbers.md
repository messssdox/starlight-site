## Why they are special

When you add up a birth date or name and reach 11, 22 or 33, numerologists stop there instead of reducing further. These double digits are thought to carry extra potential, like a stronger, louder version of the number underneath them.

## 11: The Intuitive

Eleven is a heightened [2](/library/numerology/number-2). Elevens are sensitive, intuitive and often inspiring to others without trying. They can pick up on atmospheres and feelings very strongly, which can be a gift or a strain. The lesson for 11 is to trust that sensitivity rather than fear it.

## 22: The Master Builder

Twenty-two is a heightened [4](/library/numerology/number-4). Twenty-twos combine big vision with practical skill, and can build things that help many people: organisations, buildings, movements. The lesson for 22 is to believe the vision is possible and take it one brick at a time.

## 33: The Master Teacher

Thirty-three is a heightened [6](/library/numerology/number-6), and the rarest of the three. Its path is about compassion, healing and guiding others. The lesson for 33 is to serve without carrying everyone's burdens alone.

## Living with a master number

Many numerologists say master numbers take time to grow into, so people often live as the smaller number (2, 4 or 6) for years before the master energy feels natural. If the description of 11 feels too much, read the 2 article too: both belong to you.

## Where traditions differ

Some numerologists also count 44 and higher as master numbers; most, including Starlight, stop at 33. Some never keep master numbers in a Personal Year, which is what Starlight does too.
