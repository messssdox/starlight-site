## The heart of 3

Three is the number of creativity and communication. Threes are warm, witty and full of ideas. Words, art and laughter come naturally, and they light up a room.

## In love

In love, Threes are fun, flirtatious and affectionate, needing a partner who enjoys conversation and play.

## At work

Threes thrive in writing, performing, design, teaching and anything creative or social.

## Something to watch

Threes can scatter their energy across too many projects. Their lesson is to focus their many gifts so they can fully shine.

## 3 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of creativity and fun.

## Who 3 gets on with

Three feels most at home with 6 and 9, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
