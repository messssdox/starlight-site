## The heart of 9

Nine is the number of completion. Nines see the bigger picture and want to make the world kinder. They are idealistic, artistic and broad-minded, with friends from every walk of life.

## In love

In love, Nines are romantic and giving, though they can love humanity more easily than one person up close.

## At work

Nines are drawn to charity, the arts, medicine, teaching and any work that serves others.

## Something to watch

Nines can find endings hard or give until they are empty. Their lesson is to let go gracefully and give without losing themselves.

## 9 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of completion and letting go.

## Who 9 gets on with

Nine feels most at home with 3 and 6, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
