## The heart of 8

Eight is the number of power and abundance. Eights have a gift for turning vision into results, managing people and money, and handling responsibility.

## In love

In love, Eights are generous and protective, looking for a strong, equal partner.

## At work

Eights shine in business, management, law, finance and anywhere authority is needed.

## Something to watch

Eights can become driven by work or control. Their lesson is to balance success with generosity and heart.

## 8 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of achievement and reward.

## Who 8 gets on with

Eight feels most at home with 2 and 4, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
