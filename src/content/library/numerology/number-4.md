## The heart of 4

Four is the number of foundations. Fours are hard-working, organised and honest, and they build things that last. Family, security and doing a job properly matter deeply to them.

## In love

In love, Fours are faithful and dependable, showing care through reliability and practical help.

## At work

Fours shine in building, engineering, finance, administration and craft: anywhere that values patience and precision.

## Something to watch

Fours can become rigid or resist change. Their lesson is to stay open while keeping their solid ground.

## 4 in different places

- **As your [Life Path](/library/numerology/life-path-number):** these qualities are your core nature and the road your life keeps leading you down.
- **As your Expression number:** these are your natural talents and how you go about things.
- **As your Soul Urge:** this is what you long for deep down.
- **As your Personality number:** this is how others see you at first.
- **As a [Personal Year](/library/numerology/personal-year-number):** a year of hard work and foundations.

## Who 4 gets on with

Four feels most at home with 2 and 8, its family members in numerology. See [Numerology compatibility](/library/compatibility/numerology-compatibility).
