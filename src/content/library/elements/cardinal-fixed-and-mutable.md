## Three ways of acting

| Mode | Signs | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cardinal | Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn | starts, leads, takes the initiative |
| Fixed | Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius | holds steady, deepens, sees it through |
| Mutable | Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces | adapts, connects, prepares for change |

## Following the seasons

Each season begins with a cardinal sign (the Sun enters Aries, Cancer, Libra and Capricorn at the equinoxes and solstices), carries on through a fixed sign at its height, and ends with a mutable sign as it changes into the next. In the northern hemisphere, spring begins in Aries, is fully spring in Taurus, and starts turning into summer in Gemini.

## In your chart

If most of your planets are cardinal, you may be a natural starter; fixed, a loyal finisher; mutable, a flexible go-between. Every team needs all three.
