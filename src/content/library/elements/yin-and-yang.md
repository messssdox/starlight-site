## Two halves of a whole

The famous black-and-white symbol shows two shapes swirling into each other, each holding a dot of the other's colour. Yin and yang are not opposites at war; they are partners. Night turns into day, winter into summer, rest into action.

- **Yang:** active, bright, warm, outward, expanding.
- **Yin:** receptive, dark, cool, inward, gathering.

Neither is better. Too much yang burns out; too much yin stagnates.

## In the Chinese zodiac

The animals alternate yang and yin around the wheel, starting with the yang Rat:

- **Yang:** Rat, Tiger, Dragon, Horse, Monkey, Dog
- **Yin:** Ox, Rabbit, Snake, Goat, Rooster, Pig

Years alternate too: years ending in an even number are yang, odd ones are yin. 2026 is a yang year.

## In everyday life

Yang animals and years tend to be more outgoing and action-led; yin ones more reflective and patient. A yang person can learn from yin's patience, and a yin person from yang's boldness. Many astrologers say the happiest partnerships balance the two.
