## What Chiron is

Chiron is a small icy body, sometimes called a comet or a minor planet, that orbits the Sun between Saturn and Uranus about every 50 years. Its orbit is very uneven, so it spends anywhere from under two years to about eight years in a sign.

## The myth

In Greek myth Chiron was a wise centaur, a teacher of heroes and a gifted healer. Wounded by a poisoned arrow that could not be cured, he spent his life healing others. That is why astrologers call him the wounded healer.

## What it means in your chart

Chiron's sign and house point to a tender spot: something that hurt early on, or a place where you have always felt not quite good enough. The idea is not that this wound disappears, but that by understanding it you gain compassion and insight, and often become especially good at helping others with the very same thing.

## The Chiron return

Around age 50 Chiron comes back to its birth position. Many people find this a time of making peace with the past and sharing what they have learned.
