## The planet itself

Mars, the red planet, orbits the Sun about every two years and spends around six weeks in each sign, longer when it turns retrograde. Mars rules Aries and, traditionally, Scorpio.

## What it means in your chart

Mars describes your drive: how you take action, stand up for yourself, play sport, compete and pursue what you desire. It also shows how you express anger.

## By element

- **Fire signs:** direct, fast and fearless.
- **Earth signs:** steady, determined and enduring.
- **Air signs:** strategic, argues with words.
- **Water signs:** driven by feeling, protective and persistent.

## Good to know

Mars turns retrograde about every two years for around ten weeks, a time when plans may stall and energy turns inward.
