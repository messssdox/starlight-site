## The planet itself

Neptune was discovered in 1846. It takes about 165 years to orbit the Sun, staying around fourteen years in each sign. Modern astrologers give it rulership of Pisces.

## What it means in your chart

Neptune describes your ideals, your sensitivity, your connection to music, art and spirit, and where you might be tempted to escape or see the world through rose-tinted glasses.

## By element

Neptune's sign is shared by everyone born over about fourteen years, so its house position is the more personal part.

## Good to know

Neptune's influence is subtle, like fog or music: inspiring, softening and sometimes confusing.
