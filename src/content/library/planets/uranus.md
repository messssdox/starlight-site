## The planet itself

Uranus was discovered in 1781, the first planet found with a telescope. It rolls around the Sun on its side every 84 years, spending about seven years in each sign. Modern astrologers give it rulership of Aquarius.

## What it means in your chart

Uranus describes where you need freedom and where you break the rules. It is linked with technology, science, surprise and awakening.

## By element

Because Uranus moves so slowly, its sign is shared by everyone born over about seven years. Its house position, which needs your birth time, is more personal.

## Good to know

Around age 42 Uranus sits opposite its birth position, which astrologers link with the classic mid-life urge for change.
