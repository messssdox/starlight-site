## The planet itself

Saturn, with its famous rings, takes about 29½ years to go round the Sun, spending around two and a half years in each sign. Saturn rules Capricorn and, traditionally, Aquarius.

## What it means in your chart

Saturn describes your sense of duty and structure, your fears and your long-term ambitions. It can feel like a strict teacher, but its lessons last a lifetime.

## By element

- **Fire signs:** learning to act with patience.
- **Earth signs:** learning to build securely and well.
- **Air signs:** learning to commit to people and ideas.
- **Water signs:** learning to hold feelings with strength.

## Good to know

When Saturn returns to its birth position, around ages 29 and 58, astrologers speak of the Saturn return: a big, often challenging milestone when people take stock and make serious life choices.
