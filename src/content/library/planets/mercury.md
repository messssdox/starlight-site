## The planet itself

Mercury, the closest planet to the Sun, goes round it in just 88 days. Because it never strays far from the Sun, your Mercury is always in the same sign as your Sun or one either side. Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo.

## What it means in your chart

Mercury describes how your mind works: how you take in information, solve problems, speak and write. It also covers short journeys, siblings, neighbours, and buying and selling.

## By element

- **Fire signs:** quick, enthusiastic, decisive thinkers.
- **Earth signs:** practical, careful, thorough thinkers.
- **Air signs:** curious, sociable, logical thinkers.
- **Water signs:** intuitive, imaginative, feeling thinkers.

## Good to know
