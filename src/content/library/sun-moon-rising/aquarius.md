## Aquarius at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 20 January to 18 February |
| Symbol | The Water Bearer ♒ |
| Element | Air |
| Mode | Fixed |
| Ruling planet | Uranus and Saturn |
| Opposite sign | Leo |

## Personality

Aquarius is the sign of the future. Aquarians are inventive, independent and community-minded, with a wide circle of friends and a strong sense of fairness. They are happy to be different.

## In love

In love, Aquarius needs friendship first and freedom always, and loves a partner who shares their ideals.

## At work

Aquarius thrives in technology, science, social causes, design and anything innovative.

## Something to watch

Aquarius can seem detached or stubborn about its ideas. Letting feelings show brings people closer.

## Aquarius as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Aquarius Moon:** you need what Aquarius needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Aquarius way.
- **Aquarius Rising:** people first meet you as original, friendly and idealistic, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Aquarius shares the Air element with Gemini and Libra, and they usually understand each other easily. As a fixed sign, Aquarius holds steady and sees things through.
