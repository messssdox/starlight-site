## Sagittarius at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 22 November to 21 December |
| Symbol | The Archer ♐ |
| Element | Fire |
| Mode | Mutable |
| Ruling planet | Jupiter |
| Opposite sign | Gemini |

## Personality

Sagittarius is the sign of the journey. Sagittarians love travel, learning, big ideas and big laughs. They are generous, open-minded and always looking for the meaning of things.

## In love

In love, Sagittarius is warm and fun, needing a partner who is also a friend and a fellow adventurer.

## At work

Sagittarius thrives in travel, teaching, publishing, law, philosophy and outdoor work.

## Something to watch

Sagittarius can be blunt or restless. Remembering others' feelings, and staying for the follow-through, helps.

## Sagittarius as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Sagittarius Moon:** you need what Sagittarius needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Sagittarius way.
- **Sagittarius Rising:** people first meet you as adventurous, optimistic and honest, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Sagittarius shares the Fire element with Aries and Leo, and they usually understand each other easily. As a mutable sign, Sagittarius adapts, connects and brings change.
