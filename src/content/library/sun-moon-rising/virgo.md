## Virgo at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 23 August to 22 September |
| Symbol | The Maiden ♍ |
| Element | Earth |
| Mode | Mutable |
| Ruling planet | Mercury |
| Opposite sign | Pisces |

## Personality

Virgo is the sign of service and skill. Virgos are clever, modest and hard-working, with an eye for detail and a real desire to be useful. They notice what needs doing and quietly do it.

## In love

In love, Virgo shows care through small, practical kindnesses and is loyal and attentive.

## At work

Virgo excels in health, science, editing, analysis, crafts and any work that needs precision.

## Something to watch

Virgo can worry or be too self-critical. Accepting 'good enough' sometimes is a gift to themselves.

## Virgo as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Virgo Moon:** you need what Virgo needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Virgo way.
- **Virgo Rising:** people first meet you as thoughtful, helpful and precise, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Virgo shares the Earth element with Taurus and Capricorn, and they usually understand each other easily. As a mutable sign, Virgo adapts, connects and brings change.
