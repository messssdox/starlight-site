## Libra at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 23 September to 22 October |
| Symbol | The Scales ♎ |
| Element | Air |
| Mode | Cardinal |
| Ruling planet | Venus |
| Opposite sign | Aries |

## Personality

Libra is the sign of balance and relationships. Librans are graceful, sociable and fair-minded, with a love of beauty and a talent for seeing every side of a question.

## In love

In love, Libra is romantic and attentive, and partnership is central to their happiness.

## At work

Libra thrives in law, diplomacy, design, the arts, mediation and anything involving people.

## Something to watch

Libra can find decisions hard and avoid conflict. Trusting their own judgement makes them even more fair.

## Libra as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Libra Moon:** you need what Libra needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Libra way.
- **Libra Rising:** people first meet you as charming, fair and harmonious, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Libra shares the Air element with Gemini and Aquarius, and they usually understand each other easily. As a cardinal sign, Libra starts things and takes the initiative.
