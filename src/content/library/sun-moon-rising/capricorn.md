## Capricorn at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 22 December to 19 January |
| Symbol | The Sea-Goat ♑ |
| Element | Earth |
| Mode | Cardinal |
| Ruling planet | Saturn |
| Opposite sign | Cancer |

## Personality

Capricorn is the sign of achievement. Capricorns are patient, disciplined and dependable, climbing steadily towards their goals like the mountain goat. They often have a dry sense of humour and grow more relaxed with age.

## In love

In love, Capricorn is loyal and committed, showing love by building a secure future together.

## At work

Capricorn excels in management, business, government, engineering and any long-term project.

## Something to watch

Capricorn can work too hard or hide their feelings. Making time for fun is part of real success.

## Capricorn as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Capricorn Moon:** you need what Capricorn needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Capricorn way.
- **Capricorn Rising:** people first meet you as ambitious, responsible and wise, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Capricorn shares the Earth element with Taurus and Virgo, and they usually understand each other easily. As a cardinal sign, Capricorn starts things and takes the initiative.
