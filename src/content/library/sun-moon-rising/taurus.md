## Taurus at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 20 April to 20 May |
| Symbol | The Bull ♉ |
| Element | Earth |
| Mode | Fixed |
| Ruling planet | Venus |
| Opposite sign | Scorpio |

## Personality

Taurus loves the good things in life: good food, comfort, nature, music and people they can rely on. Taureans are calm, patient and practical, and once they have decided something, they see it through.

## In love

In love, Taurus is affectionate, loyal and devoted, showing love through touch, gifts and steady presence.

## At work

Taurus shines in finance, food, gardening, design, music and any work that builds lasting value.

## Something to watch

Taurus can be stubborn and slow to change. Trying something new now and then keeps life fresh.

## Taurus as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Taurus Moon:** you need what Taurus needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Taurus way.
- **Taurus Rising:** people first meet you as steady, sensual and loyal, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Taurus shares the Earth element with Virgo and Capricorn, and they usually understand each other easily. As a fixed sign, Taurus holds steady and sees things through.
