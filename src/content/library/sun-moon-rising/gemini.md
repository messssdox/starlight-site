## Gemini at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 21 May to 20 June |
| Symbol | The Twins ♊ |
| Element | Air |
| Mode | Mutable |
| Ruling planet | Mercury |
| Opposite sign | Sagittarius |

## Personality

Gemini is the sign of conversation and ideas. Geminis are quick, funny and interested in everything, with a gift for connecting people and information. They are wonderful company and learn new things easily.

## In love

In love, Gemini needs mental connection most of all: a partner they can talk to for hours and never get bored with.

## At work

Gemini thrives in writing, media, teaching, sales, communications and anything with variety.

## Something to watch

Gemini can scatter its energy or seem changeable. Finishing what they start helps their ideas come to life.

## Gemini as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Gemini Moon:** you need what Gemini needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Gemini way.
- **Gemini Rising:** people first meet you as curious, witty and sociable, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Gemini shares the Air element with Libra and Aquarius, and they usually understand each other easily. As a mutable sign, Gemini adapts, connects and brings change.
