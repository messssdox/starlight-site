## What the Sun shows

In astrology the Sun stands for your centre: your sense of self, your vitality, your pride and purpose. Its sign describes the flavour of that core self. It is the part of you that shines when you feel most yourself.

## How it is worked out

Over a year the Sun appears to travel through all twelve signs, spending about a month in each. The dates shift by a day or so from year to year, so if your birthday is on the change between two signs (a "cusp"), the exact time and year decide which sign you are. Starlight works this out precisely.

## Cusps

People born on the day the Sun changes sign sometimes say they are "on the cusp" and feel like a blend. Astronomically you are always one sign or the other, but neighbouring planets in your chart may well bring in the next sign's flavour too.
