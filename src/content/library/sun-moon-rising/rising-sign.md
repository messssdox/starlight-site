## What the Rising sign shows

Think of your Rising sign as your front door. It is the first thing people meet: your manner, your style, sometimes even your looks. It also describes how you instinctively approach anything new, from a job interview to a party.

## Why it needs your birth time

As the Earth turns, all twelve signs rise over the eastern horizon in turn, one about every two hours. So two people born on the same day can have completely different Rising signs. Your birth time and place pin it down.

## The start of your chart

The Rising sign also marks the start of the first house, and so sets up all twelve [houses](/library/houses-aspects/the-twelve-houses) of your chart. That is why charts without a birth time leave the houses out.

## Rising signs in brief

- **Aries Rising:** direct, energetic, ready for action.
- **Taurus Rising:** calm, warm and unhurried.
- **Gemini Rising:** chatty, curious and quick.
- **Cancer Rising:** gentle, caring and protective.
- **Leo Rising:** sunny, confident and stylish.
- **Virgo Rising:** modest, neat and helpful.
- **Libra Rising:** charming, polite and easy to like.
- **Scorpio Rising:** intense, private and magnetic.
- **Sagittarius Rising:** friendly, open and adventurous.
- **Capricorn Rising:** composed, capable and serious.
- **Aquarius Rising:** friendly but a little different, original.
- **Pisces Rising:** dreamy, soft and sympathetic.

## Don't know your time?

Starlight will still give you a full reading of your Sun, Moon and planets by sign. If you later find your birth time, the Rising sign and houses will add a new layer.
