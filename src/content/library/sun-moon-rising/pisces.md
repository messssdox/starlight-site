## Pisces at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 19 February to 20 March |
| Symbol | The Fish ♓ |
| Element | Water |
| Mode | Mutable |
| Ruling planet | Neptune and Jupiter |
| Opposite sign | Virgo |

## Personality

Pisces is the last sign of the zodiac and holds a little of all the others. Pisceans are sensitive, artistic and deeply kind, with a strong imagination and intuition.

## In love

In love, Pisces is romantic and devoted, giving with an open heart.

## At work

Pisces flourishes in the arts, music, healing, care, spiritual work and anything that uses imagination.

## Something to watch

Pisces can drift or absorb other people's moods. Clear boundaries keep their gentle gifts safe.

## Pisces as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Pisces Moon:** you need what Pisces needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Pisces way.
- **Pisces Rising:** people first meet you as compassionate, imaginative and gentle, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Pisces shares the Water element with Cancer and Scorpio, and they usually understand each other easily. As a mutable sign, Pisces adapts, connects and brings change.
