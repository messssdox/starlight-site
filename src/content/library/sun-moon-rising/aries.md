## Aries at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 21 March to 19 April |
| Symbol | The Ram ♈ |
| Element | Fire |
| Mode | Cardinal |
| Ruling planet | Mars |
| Opposite sign | Libra |

## Personality

Aries is the first sign of the zodiac, and Aries people love to be first too. They are energetic, honest and courageous, quick to start things and quick to stand up for themselves and others. Life with an Aries is never dull.

## In love

In love, Aries is passionate, playful and wholehearted, enjoying the chase and loving a partner with spirit.

## At work

Aries does best in roles that need drive and quick decisions: leadership, sport, emergency services, sales and starting new ventures.

## Something to watch

Aries can be impatient and act before thinking. Slowing down for a moment turns their courage into wisdom.

## Aries as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Aries Moon:** you need what Aries needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Aries way.
- **Aries Rising:** people first meet you as brave, direct and full of energy, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Aries shares the Fire element with Leo and Sagittarius, and they usually understand each other easily. As a cardinal sign, Aries starts things and takes the initiative.
