## Scorpio at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 23 October to 21 November |
| Symbol | The Scorpion ♏ |
| Element | Water |
| Mode | Fixed |
| Ruling planet | Pluto and Mars |
| Opposite sign | Taurus |

## Personality

Scorpio is the sign of depth and transformation. Scorpios feel everything strongly, see beneath the surface and are fiercely loyal to the people they trust. They are determined and resilient, able to rise again after hard times.

## In love

In love, Scorpio is passionate and all-in, wanting honesty and a deep bond rather than something casual.

## At work

Scorpio excels in research, psychology, medicine, investigation, finance and anything that needs focus.

## Something to watch

Scorpio can be secretive or hold grudges. Letting go of old hurts frees their great strength.

## Scorpio as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Scorpio Moon:** you need what Scorpio needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Scorpio way.
- **Scorpio Rising:** people first meet you as intense, loyal and perceptive, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Scorpio shares the Water element with Cancer and Pisces, and they usually understand each other easily. As a fixed sign, Scorpio holds steady and sees things through.
