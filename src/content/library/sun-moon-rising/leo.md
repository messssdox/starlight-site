## Leo at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Dates (Sun sign) | 23 July to 22 August |
| Symbol | The Lion ♌ |
| Element | Fire |
| Mode | Fixed |
| Ruling planet | The Sun |
| Opposite sign | Aquarius |

## Personality

Leo is the sign of the heart. Leos are big-hearted, creative and fun, with a natural presence that lights up a room. They are loyal friends who love to celebrate the people around them.

## In love

In love, Leo is romantic, generous and proud of their partner, and they flourish with appreciation and affection.

## At work

Leo shines in performance, leadership, teaching, the arts and anywhere they can inspire others.

## Something to watch

Leo can need a lot of attention or be hurt by criticism. Remembering that their worth doesn't depend on applause brings real confidence.

## Leo as your Moon or Rising sign

- **Leo Moon:** you need what Leo needs to feel safe, and your feelings show in a Leo way.
- **Leo Rising:** people first meet you as warm, generous and confident, even if your Sun sign is quite different.

## Element and mode

Leo shares the Fire element with Aries and Sagittarius, and they usually understand each other easily. As a fixed sign, Leo holds steady and sees things through.
